Budgets

What was planned, and what it has cost so far

A budget covers a period and holds one line per budgetary position or analytic account. Each line shows the planned amount, the theoretical amount expected by today, the actual amount posted and the achievement.

  • ✓  Draft, confirmed, validated, done and cancelled, with the lines locked once confirmed.
  • ✓  Expense and revenue lines, spread over the period or expected in full on a planned date.
  • ✓  Copy a budget to the next period, or split its lines by month or by quarter.

A budget with its lines and the amounts achieved.

Positions

Group the accounts the way you steer the business

A budgetary position is a named group of accounts, and it is what a budget line is measured against.

  • ✓  As many positions as you need, each with its own accounts.
  • ✓  Combine a position with an analytic account to budget a project or a department.

Budgetary positions and their accounts.

Control

Know before the money is spent, not after

Vendor bills that would push a line over its plan show a warning banner while they are still drafts.

  • ✓  Notify the responsible when a line reaches a share of its plan, and email them when it goes over.
  • ✓  Block the documents over budget for everyone but the accounting managers.
  • ✓  An Over Budget filter on the lines.
  • ✓  With the Budget Control of Purchases add-on, the same on purchase orders, plus the committed amounts.
Reporting

Budget against actual, printed or analysed

A Budget vs Actual PDF for the file, and a budget analysis in pivot, list and graph views for the screen.

  • ✓  By position, by analytic account or by period.
  • ✓  Exported to a spreadsheet from the pivot.
