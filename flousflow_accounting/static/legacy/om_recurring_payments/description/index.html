Templates

Describe the entry once

A template holds the lines of the entry: the accounts, the partner, the amounts and the analytic distribution.

  • ✓  As many templates as you have repeating commitments.
  • ✓  Any journal, any accounts, with taxes and analytic distribution.

Schedules

Then say how often it repeats

A recurring payment takes a template, a start date, a frequency and an end, and generates the entries on time.

  • ✓  Daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly.
  • ✓  Until a date, or without end.
  • ✓  The entries generated are listed on the record, so you can see what has been posted and what is coming.
  • ✓  With Auto-Post Entries they are posted as well; without it they wait in draft for you to check.
