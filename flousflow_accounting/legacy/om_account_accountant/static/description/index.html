Accounting Suite for Odoo 20 Community

Accounting reports and workflows in one install: statements, ledgers, reconciliation, cash management, assets, budgets, follow-up, and dashboards.

Accounting reports

Prepare financial statements and review accounting activity by company, date, journal, and entry status.

  • Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss, and Cash Flow Statement.
  • General Ledger, Partner Ledger, Trial Balance, and Aged Partner Balance.
  • Tax Report, Open Items, Journal Audit, and customer or supplier statements.
  • PDF and spreadsheet exports where supported by each report.

Daily accounting work

  • Bank and cash reconciliation, statement import, and internal account transfers.
  • Assets and depreciation, budgets, recurring entries, and fiscal-year controls.
  • Customer follow-up, cash forecasting, currency updates, and revaluation.
  • Vendor-bill data extraction processed on the Odoo server.
  • Accounting dashboards and working-file balance review.

Requirements and data handling

  • Odoo 20.0 Community Edition and the standard Odoo modules listed in the manifests.
  • For on-premise PDF rendering, install the Odoo-compatible wkhtmltopdf build with patched Qt.
  • Vendor-bill OCR requires Tesseract and Poppler on the Odoo host. Documents are processed locally.
  • Currency-rate updates contact the provider selected by the administrator when the feature is configured.

Each bundled addon retains its own manifest and license. Review those licenses before redistributing or modifying individual components.
