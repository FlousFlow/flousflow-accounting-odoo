Reading

Upload the bill, check it, post it

Use the Upload button of the vendor bills, drop the files on the list, or let your suppliers email the purchase journal: each PDF or image becomes a draft bill, already filled in, with the document beside it.

  • ✓  The vendor, recognized by its tax number (VAT, TRN, GSTIN, USt-IdNr...), its bank account, its email or its name, and never by your own company printed as the customer.
  • ✓  The bill number, the bill date, the due date, the currency and the purchase order number.
  • ✓  The untaxed amount, the taxes and the total, checked against each other, and the purchase tax of the rate printed on the bill.
  • ✓  The lines of the table (description, quantity, unit price), kept only when they add up to the untaxed amount; otherwise one line with the right amount.
  • ✓  Bills in English, French, German and Spanish, any date format and any thousands separator.
  • ✓  A banner names the fields to check. Nothing is ever posted on its own.

Learning

Every bill you post teaches the next one

When you post a bill that was read, with your corrections, the module remembers what its vendor's bills look like. The second bill of a vendor comes out right where the first one needed a hand.

  • ✓  The labels the vendor prints before its number, its dates and its total, whatever its wording.
  • ✓  How it writes its dates: is 04/03 the 4th of March or the 3rd of April?
  • ✓  The account, the taxes and the product its bills are booked on, and whether to keep its lines.
  • ✓  Its tax numbers, bank accounts and letterhead, to recognize a vendor that has no tax number in your database.

On your server

Free, private, and nothing to subscribe to

No credits, no cloud service, no API key: your bills never leave your server.

  • ✓  The PDFs made by an invoicing software are read from their own text: nothing to install.
  • ✓  The scans and the photos are read by Tesseract, the open-source OCR engine: sudo apt install tesseract-ocr poppler-utils, plus a language pack per language of your bills (tesseract-ocr-fra, tesseract-ocr-deu...).
  • ✓  Electronic invoices (Factur-X, UBL, Peppol) keep being imported exactly by Odoo, from their XML.
  • ✓  Read on upload, or only on demand with the Read the Bill button, per company.
  • ✓  Sample bills to try it with are in the module's tests/samples folder: US, German, French, Indian and UAE layouts, a scan and a photo.
